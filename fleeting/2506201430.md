- **domain name system (DNS)** - protocol that matches domain names to 
  respective IP addresses; a system that is more accessible than memorizing a 
  bunch of numbers (IP address)
- Within DNS, part of the URL that is typically typed and remembered is called 
  the **fully qualified domain name (FQDN)**. The FQDN is important to how the 
  DNS is able to recursively search the desired server. It is read from right
  to left in which:
  represented by `.` 
  - the rightmost of the FQDN is the **top-level domain (TLD)** aka root 
    servers: `.com`, `.org`, `.us`, etc.
  - the **second-level-domain (SLD)** is the registered domain itself, such as:
    linkedin.com
  - the subdomain refers to the resources within the server tied to the
    registered domain: learning.linkedin.com
